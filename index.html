Setup in minutes

Three steps to your
first live environment

A single config file. No infrastructure decisions. No DevOps background required. Just connect and ship.

01
Connect your repo

Authorize embr on your Git provider. Select the repo you want to deploy. That's the whole setup.

embr connect
02
Push your code

Push to main to deploy production. Open a PR to get a preview. embr detects your framework and handles the rest.

git push origin main
03
Share the link

Every environment gets a unique URL. Comment it in your PR, share it with stakeholders, or test it yourself — it's always live.
